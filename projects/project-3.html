One-hand torque-assist socket adapter

TorqueDrive is a one-hand torque-assist socket adapter developed through the engineering design process. The project moved from problem definition and concept development into CAD, physical prototyping, testing, and product iteration. The prototype was tested to approximately 50 ft-lb.

TorqueDrive was presented to industry professionals and regional business leaders through the Pikes Peak regional industry capstone program. The project received formal recognition from the Colorado Springs Chamber & EDC and the regional industry panel.
